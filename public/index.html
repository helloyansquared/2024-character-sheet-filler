Convert your D&D Beyond character PDF to the 2024 character sheet

D&D Beyond's Export to PDF still gives you its older multi-page layout. Drop that PDF here and get the official two-page 2024 character sheet, filled in and still editable. Free, and it all happens in your browser.

  1. 1

    Your character PDF

  2. 2

You can add both PDFs at once.

  3. 3

    Check your spells

    The 2024 sheet lists cantrips and prepared spells (30 rows). Anything beyond that goes on an extra page.

  4. 4

    Download

    Add your character PDF (step 1) and the blank 2024 sheet (step 2) first.

Your PDF is read and filled in this browser tab. It is never uploaded. We only count anonymous steps (like “a sheet was made”), nothing about your character.

How it works

  1. Reads your export. D&D Beyond's PDF keeps every value in a form field. We read those fields: name, class and subclass, ability scores, saves, skills, attacks, features, equipment, coins, spells and slots.
  2. Fills the official sheet. You add the free 2024 sheet from D&D Beyond and we type each value into its matching box. Long text shrinks to fit; whatever still doesn't fit goes on an extra page, so nothing is dropped.
  3. Stays editable. The result is the same form-fillable sheet, so you can fix anything in any PDF app before you print.

Checked against real exports: levels 1 to 8, Bard, Cleric, Druid, Fighter, Paladin, 2014 and 2024 rules, and an export from 2023. Not checked yet: level 10+, Warlock pact slots and multiclass spellcasters. If yours looks off, tell us (a PDF helps a lot).

FAQ

Why doesn't D&D Beyond export the 2024 sheet itself?

Its Export to PDF button still produces D&D Beyond's own multi-page layout, even for characters built with the 2024 rules. Players have been asking for the 2024 layout on the D&D Beyond forums since the 2024 Player's Handbook came out. Until that changes, this tool fills the official sheet from the export you already have.

Is my character file uploaded anywhere?

No. The page reads your PDF and builds the new one inside your browser. The only network requests are loading this page and an anonymous step counter with no file contents. See privacy.

Why do I have to download the blank sheet myself?

The 2024 sheet belongs to Wizards of the Coast, so we don't host or hand out copies, and D&D Beyond's file server only lets its own site load it directly. So you download the free, fillable version from the D&D Beyond Free Rules (the link in step 1) and add it next to your character PDF. Keep it and you can reuse it for every character. The first 2024 sheet that came with the Player's Handbook isn't fillable, so use the Free Rules one.

Which spells end up on the sheet?

The 2024 sheet has 30 rows for cantrips and prepared spells. If your list fits, everything is ticked for you. Prepared casters like Clerics, Druids and Paladins often export their whole class list, because D&D Beyond's PDF doesn't say which spells you prepared today. Then we tick cantrips and always-prepared spells, and you tick the rest. Unticked spells aren't printed; ticked spells past row 30 go on an extra page.

What about features, feats and long backstories?

Class features, species traits and feats are listed by name, because the 2024 sheet's boxes are small. Long text shrinks to fit, down to a readable minimum. Anything that still doesn't fit goes on a continuation page in full.

Can I edit the result?

Yes. It's the official form-fillable sheet with your values in the fields, so any PDF app that supports forms (Acrobat Reader, Preview, most phone PDF apps) can change them.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. Use the “Choose” buttons instead of dragging. Open the downloaded PDF in a PDF app to see and edit the fields; some built-in phone previewers don't show form fields.

Something came out wrong. What now?

Email hello@yansquared.com or open an issue on GitHub. Say what's wrong, and attach the exported PDF if you're happy to share it. Level 10+, Warlock and multiclass examples are especially welcome.
