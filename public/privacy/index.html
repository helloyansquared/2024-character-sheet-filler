2024 Character Sheet Filler

Privacy

Your files stay in your browser. The character PDF you choose is read inside this browser tab, and the filled 2024 sheet is built there and saved straight to your device. Neither file is uploaded to us or anyone else.

What the page loads

  • This site's own pages and scripts (hosted on Cloudflare Pages). The PDF library (pdf-lib, MIT licence) is served from this site too.
  • Nothing else. The blank 2024 character sheet is something you download yourself from D&D Beyond (the link on the main page) and add from your device.

What we count

To know whether the tool is used and where it breaks, the page sends a few anonymous step counts to this site's own counter: that a visit happened and which site sent it (for example “google” or “direct”), whether the blank sheet loaded, whether a character PDF was read (or a short error code like “no-fields”), and whether a sheet was made. No file contents, names, character details, cookies or IDs are sent. After a sheet is made you may see one optional question, “How did you find this site?”, which you can skip. If you answer, the choice you tap is sent, plus the short optional text you type into “What did you ask or search for?” (up to 200 characters; please leave out personal details).

To avoid counting the same thing twice, the page keeps a few small markers in your browser’s session and local storage (for example, that you already answered the question). They contain no ID and never leave your browser.

The counts are stored in a database at our host, Cloudflare. Cloudflare works out the country from the connection. Your IP address is only held briefly in memory to stop spam and is never stored. Records are deleted after 180 days.
